# **Business Insights**



**Revenue Analysis**

\- Revenue was heavily concentrated in major cities, with Sao Paulo generating the highest sales revenue by a significant margin.

\- Rio de Janeiro ranked second, but with considerably lower revenue compared to Sao Paulo.

\- This indicates strong market dominance in large metropolitan regions.



\---



**Operational Performance**

\- The business achieved an excellent delivery success rate of 97.02%.

\- Canceled orders represented only 0.63% of total orders.

\- These metrics reflect efficient logistics and fulfillment operations.



\---



**Customer Retention Analysis**

\- The Repeat Purchase Rate was only 3.11%, indicating that most customers purchased only once.

\- Cohort analysis showed a sharp drop in customer retention after the first month.

\- Retention rates remained relatively low across most cohorts, suggesting weak long-term customer engagement.



\---



**Customer Segmentation (RFM Analysis)**

\- Most customers belonged to the following segments:

&#x20; - Promising

&#x20; - Requires Attention



\- Loyal and VIP customers represented a very small percentage of the customer base.

\- Only 718 customers were classified as VIP Customers, but they generated strong monetary value and higher purchase frequency.



\---



**Sales Behavior**

\- The Average Order Value (AOV) reached 137.75, while the average product price was 120.65.

\- This suggests that many customers purchased multiple products within the same order.



\---



**Customer Growth Trends**

\- The number of active customers increased steadily throughout 2017 and 2018.

\- Business growth appears to be driven primarily by customer acquisition rather than customer retention.



\---



**Customer Lifetime Insights**

\- Some customers remained active for more than 300 days, showing strong long-term engagement.

\- However, a large percentage of customers made only one purchase before becoming inactive.

\- This highlights a major gap between loyal customers and one-time buyers.



\---



**Final Business Conclusion**

\- The business demonstrates strong operational efficiency and continuous customer acquisition growth.

\- However, retention and repeat purchase behavior remain relatively weak.

\- Improving customer retention strategies and loyalty programs could significantly increase long-term revenue and customer lifetime value.
